### Structural decision baked into this model: free shipping
Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee applies to shipping charged to the buyer anyway, so charging shipping separately saves nothing — and free shipping improves search placement. **All prices below are shipping-inclusive.**

### The working formula
```
Net profit = 0.905 × Price − Production − Shipping − $0.45
             (0.905 = 1 − 6.5% transaction − 3% processing)
             ($0.45 = $0.20 listing + $0.25 processing flat)
```
**Absolute break-even price: $15.38.** Below this you lose money on every sale.

- **Standard $26** sits in the lower-middle of the band. Competitive for a shop with no reviews yet.
- **Premium $32** is defensible on product quality but aggressive without social proof.

## ⚠️ THE OFFSITE ADS TRAP

Etsy Offsite Ads take **15%** of the order (shops under $10k lifetime) or 12% (over $10k). It only fires when an offsite ad wins the sale — but it stacks on top of everything above.

| Price | Normal net | Net if offsite ad drove the sale | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| $22.00 | $5.99 | **$2.69** | −55% |
| $26.00 | $9.61 | **$5.71** | −41% |
| $32.00 | $15.04 | **$10.24** | −32% |

**At $22, more than half your profit disappears on any offsite-attributed sale.** This is the single strongest argument against Conservative pricing.

Your shop is under $10k, so **offsite ads are optional — you can opt out.** Recommendation: opt out at launch, revisit once margins are proven.

## ⚠️ ADVERTISING VIABILITY — THE HARD FINDING

Break-even CPA at Standard pricing is **$9.61**. Target CPA to keep half your profit is **$4.81**.

Etsy Ads typically run $0.20–0.50 CPC at 1–3% conversion, implying a **CPA of $10–25**.

**At $26 single-bag pricing, Etsy Ads are likely break-even to loss-making.** Per the collection's own decision rules — *test before scaling, no large ad budgets without evidence* — this means:

1. Launch with **organic SEO only**. The listing package is already built for it.
2. If you test ads, cap at **$3–5/day** and judge on measured CPA, not impressions.
3. Ads become viable on the **$50 bundle** ($19.76 break-even CPA) far sooner than on singles.

## When zero sales becomes a signal

Zero sales on day 0, with no ad spend and a brand-new shop still building
search visibility, is normal and not a concern. It would become a 🟡 Watch
per `agents/analyticsdir/kpi-and-alerts.md`'s zero-activity trigger only
after 3 consecutive logged periods of zero orders **while traffic data
shows views/clicks are actually happening** — that combination (traffic
without conversion) is the real signal, not the absence of sales alone.
Right now there isn't even traffic data yet, so no alert is warranted or
possible. Re-evaluate once the first few days of Stats are logged.
